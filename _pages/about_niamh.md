<h2 id="niamh">Niamh Fennelly</h2>

#### <i>PhD Student</i>

I completed my BSc in Applied and Computational Mathematics at University College Dublin in 2023 and am now a third-year PhD student in Mathematical Sciences at UCD, funded by the Research Ireland Centre for Research Training in Foundations of Data Science. 

I specialise in mathematical neuroscience with a focus on synaptic plasticity and low-dimensional models of neural networks. Specifically, I implement and analyse biophysically inspired plasticity rules within the next-generation neural mass framework. I am currently extending this to next-generation neural field models to investigate plasticity-driven spatial patterning and spatiotemporal dynamics. My research interests include mean-field theory, bifurcation analysis, multistability and chaos.
